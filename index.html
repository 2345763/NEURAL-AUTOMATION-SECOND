Solutions · how agentic systems work

From idea to intelligence.

An agent isn't a chatbot. It reads the input, reasons about it, uses your tools and finishes the job. Pick a scenario and follow the data.

  1. InputA new enquiry lands on WhatsApp: “Need pricing for 40 units, delivery by Friday.”
  2. AI reasoningThe agent reads intent, quantity and urgency, then scores the lead against your ideal-customer rules.
  3. ToolsIt checks the CRM for history, the price sheet for a quote and the calendar for free slots.
  4. AutomationThe CRM record is created, the quote drafted and the sales owner assigned — no copy-paste.
  5. ActionA reply goes out with the quote and a booking link. Hot leads ping the team instantly.
  6. Business resultEvery lead answered in minutes and qualified the same way, every time.

Questions

Before you send a brief.

Straight answers. Anything else — ask on WhatsApp.

What does Neural Automation build?

AI agents and agentic workflows, business automation, custom software, premium websites and 3D/WebGL experiences, mobile apps, and the digital growth systems that bring in customers. Most projects combine two or three of these — which is why they're built under one roof.

Do you build AI agents for businesses?

Yes. We design and build AI agents, LLM applications, RAG systems, multi-agent workflows and AI copilots that connect to the tools you already use — with human approval on anything sensitive.

Can you automate our CRM, WhatsApp and lead follow-ups?

Yes. Lead capture and qualification, CRM updates, WhatsApp messaging, reporting and hand-offs can run through API integrations, workflow tools and RPA — so your team stops copy-pasting between apps.

Do you develop 3D and WebGL websites?

Yes. We build with Next.js, React, Three.js, WebGL and GSAP, and engineer every experience for performance, accessibility and SEO — including graceful fallbacks for low-power devices.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We're based in Faridabad, Haryana and work remotely with businesses in India and worldwide. Indian projects are quoted in rupees; international engagements in USD.

How much does a project cost?

Website development and social media marketing start at ₹25,000. App development starts at ₹50,000. Custom packages combine several services and are quoted against a written brief — the final number depends on requirements, complexity and timeline.
